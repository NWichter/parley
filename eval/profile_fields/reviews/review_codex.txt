The main issue is that several fields turn observations into stronger claims than the evidence supports. A deterministic policy needs those distinctions preserved.

### 1. Concrete problems

- **Approval threshold:** “changes above 2%” does not establish the baseline, direction, scope, or whether approval concerns the cumulative change. Capping each round at 2% could still exceed the approval limit.
- **Levers:** valuing volume does not establish willingness to accept a binding commitment in exchange for a concession. Contract terms and past quote terms also do not establish preferences.
- **Direction:** split `effective_date_later` into delaying a supplier-requested increase and delaying a buyer-requested reduction. Split call-off flexibility from longer lead time; specify who gains flexibility. `tooling` needs payer, ownership, and payment purpose.
- **Unsupported psychological/financial judgments:** faster-payment requests do not establish financial distress; outdated costing does not establish an “uninformed owner”; none of these sources establishes patience. Stable financial ratios do not prove absence of liquidity needs.
- **Contact versus authority:** an inside-sales contact is not necessarily the decision maker. The role enum also omits sales director and committee.
- **Hedges:** a series list loses material, duration, coverage, and scope. Fixed copper prices do not establish that every copper-related series is irrelevant. Hedges also do not prove that the supplier’s entire cost base is fixed.
- **Price freeze:** distinguish a supplier’s refusal to negotiate reductions from contractual price validity. A calendar-year fixed-price clause is not evidence of a head-office reduction ban.
- **Unknown semantics:** use `null` for unknown. An empty list means no extracted items, not proven absence of preferences or hedges. `false` and `"none"` require affirmative evidence.
- **Evidence validity:** substring matching establishes quote fidelity, not that the quote entails the value. Repeated notes may repeat one assertion rather than independently corroborate it.
- **Outcome leakage:** exclude existing profiles and historical asked/settled/awarded values. Under the strict “no outcome data” requirement, also exclude “accepted below full cost to keep the volume”: it describes a negotiation outcome.
- **Missing useful facts:** current request, contractual price validity and notice conditions, hedge expiry, committee cadence, accelerated-payment offers, and volume-based account prioritization.

### 2. Revised schema

Every atomic assertion uses `{value, evidence, confidence}`. Unknown scalars use `{value:null, evidence:[], confidence:null}`. Lists contain individually evidenced assertions; `[]` means “nothing supported.” Dates retain source precision. Record source date separately from any stated validity period.

`high` = explicitly supported; `medium` = supported but interpretation remains; `low` = unresolved ambiguity, excluded from automatic policy actions. Confidence measures support for the assertion, not whether the supplier’s assertion is true.

| Field | Value shape | Extraction and policy rule |
|---|---|---|
| `approval_rule` | `{threshold_pct, comparator, direction, baseline, scope, approver, cadence}` | Extract stated components only; unknown components remain null. Never assume a per-round threshold or bypass approval by splitting changes. |
| `negotiating_roles` | List of `{role, relationship}` | Roles include owner, KAM, inside_sales, sales_director, export, committee, other. Relationship distinguishes observed participant, designated contact, and stated approver. |
| `lever_signals` | List of `{lever, stance, condition, parameters}` | Stance: requested, offered, explicitly_open, rejected. Require an explicit signal about the term; attach conditions to the same assertion. Rejection is limited to its stated scope and time. |
| `volume_priority` | Stated account-selection preference | Record preference for volume separately from willingness to trade a binding commitment. |
| `capacity_constraint` | `{state, scope, period}` | State: constrained or explicitly_unconstrained. Require an explicit capacity statement; shutdown dates and ordinary lead times are insufficient. |
| `price_reduction_restriction` | `{scope, reason, until}` | Require an explicit restriction on reductions. Contractual fixed pricing goes below. |
| `input_price_cover` | List of `{input, mechanism, coverage, start, end}` | Extract material/energy, fixed-price or forward mechanism, and stated coverage period. Unknown coverage remains null. |
| `cost_tracking_observation` | List of factual observations | Record spreadsheet age, tracking lapse, or costing responsibility; infer no competence label or communication tactic. |
| `round_limit` | `{max_rounds, deadline, stated_preference}` | Require an explicit statement about rounds or negotiation duration; otherwise null. Replaces patience. |
| `liquidity_statement` | Explicit statement of financing/cash pressure | Faster-payment requests belong in lever signals; they alone do not populate this field. |
| `price_terms` | `{fixed_period, notice_days, notice_anchor}` | Preserve contractual wording and anchor. Do not equate a notice period with unilateral permission to change prices. |
| `current_price_request` | `{part, change_pct, old_price, new_price, currency, effective_date}` | Extract the current request, separate from contract constraints and historical outcomes. |

Use precise lever identifiers: `buyer_payment_days_shorter`, `buyer_payment_days_longer`, `buyer_accepts_longer_lead_time`, `supplier_calloff_flexibility`, `contract_term_longer`, `buyer_binding_volume_commitment`, `index_linked_price`, `supplier_increase_effective_later`, `buyer_reduction_effective_later`, `buyer_relaxes_ppm_target`, `buyer_waives_complaint_fee`, `buyer_funds_tooling`. Add explicit cash-discount parameters to payment signals.

Keep **index mapping separate from extraction**: map the evidenced input to applicable part cost drivers using a maintained mapping. A hedge should qualify an index argument, not automatically prohibit mentioning that index.
