What Parley does

Reads. Negotiates. Signs or walks.

  1. 01 · Read

    The e-mail, the quote PDF and three years of history with this supplier.

  2. 02 · Negotiate

    Up to ten e-mails. Every offer becomes one total cost over the contract term.

  3. 03 · Close

    Accept the cheapest offer that keeps every must-have, or walk away when the alternative is cheaper.

ParleySheet 08/10Rev D

Real suppliers

Built for suppliers it has never met.

  • No memorised bots. No rule names a supplier; a test fails if one does.
  • Reads the supplier live: step sizes, final-offer timing, signal sentences.
  • Safe by construction: accepts only below the cheapest opening, every must-have kept, cheaper than the alternative.
  • Tested on the unknown: held-out generated cases, suppliers voiced by Claude, Gemini, GPT and DeepSeek.
  • Reads new data: Sunday’s release (supplier news and letters) became dossier facts: cash need, free capacity, cited by ID.

Rules it plays by

  1. Round one is the negotiation.
  2. Lead with the cost drivers that fell.
  3. Never push after a final offer.
  4. Walk away when the alternative is cheaper.
